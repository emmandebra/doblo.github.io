Our Profile

Registered, Credentialed, and Built to Serve

At Doblo Gonno Community Clinic & Maternity, we believe that high-quality medical attention should not be a luxury. Established as a licensed healthcare hub in Accra, Ghana, our focus remains firmly fixed on the overall wellness of families within our community.

"Your Health, Our Concern" drives every procedure, diagnosis, and delivery under our roof.

NHIS Accredited Partner

We proudly honor National Health Insurance Scheme coverages to keep medical bills manageable.

Why Our Community Trusts Us

Uninterrupted Operations Emergency services and maternity wings are fully manned 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Comprehensive Medical Setup From standard outpatient diagnostics to specialized theater surgeries, avoid hospital jumping.

Empathetic Care Team Professional midwives, general practitioners, and laboratory specialists treating you like kin.

Clinical Capabilities

Our Comprehensive Services

Every medical discipline needed to preserve and protect your household's health is active right here.

OPD (Out-Patient Department)

Daily walk-in consultations, primary triage, preventative medicine, and general health screenings.

In-Patient Services

Comfortable recovery wings, including VIP Wards equipped for optimal patient rest and continuous monitoring.

Surgical Theatre

Advanced surgical intervention room handles Caesarian Sections, Fibroids removals, Hernia repairs, and more.

Maternity & Antenatal (ANC)

Complete prenatal support schedules, secure guided deliveries, and attentive post-natal checks.

Paediatrics

Specialized medical tracking and diagnostic attention tailored exclusively for infants and maturing children.

Emergency Services

Equipped for rapid crisis management, stabilizing urgent health complications at any time of night or day.

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Precise on-site blood panels, specimen analysis, and ultra-sound scans for immediate medical insights.

Special Clinics

Targeted chronic management and wellness tracks covering Diabetes, Hypertension, and Stroke rehabilitation.

Pharmacy & Comprehensive Checks

Stocked with genuine, accurately measured therapeutics along with full physical packages and home care visits.
